# Literature audit and contribution scope

Checked 5 September 2026 against primary papers, author-maintained research pages, publisher records, and official technical documentation. Searches used public concepts: adaptive exact predicates, convex first-moment bounds, finite-vector majorization, query-dependent KV selection, local attention certificates, state composition, and exact-real next-token certification. No private repository text or research results were submitted in search queries. This is a targeted comparison, not an exhaustive priority determination.

The defensible claim is a specific construction: residuals `N - tD` tested at exact output-cell boundaries, mergeable moment summaries arranged in an append-only refinement forest, and acceptance at the complete persistent-write boundary. The integer-row refinement improves the supplied algorithm without adding summary metadata. Its convex extremal argument belongs to established moment/majorization mathematics; neither that argument nor runtime certification in general is claimed as a first invention.

## Unresolved novelty and evidence questions

The targeted search found substantial related work for each major ingredient. It did not establish whether the exact residual/moment/write-frontier combination has appeared elsewhere, so the manuscript avoids “first,” “novel exact attention,” and exhaustive priority claims. A paper submission should extend the comparison through citations of these primary works, inspect closely related implementations, and compare guarantees at matching numerical and state boundaries.

The finite-cardinality refinement is a proved improvement of this repository's bound, not evidence of a previously unknown convex inequality or broad pretrained usefulness. It is sharp only when the retained constraints are count, sum, value range, and an independently feasible common weight box. Discrete BF16 values, E24 weights, and key/value correlations may yield a smaller feasible set. CUDA initially retains the continuous chord because its first moment is an interval; the exact-floor formula cannot be substituted blindly when that interval crosses an integer boundary.
